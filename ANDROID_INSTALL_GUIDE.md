# Sonance Android Installation Guide 📱🎵

This guide walks you through installing and running **Sonance** on Android devices running **Android 8.0 (Oreo)** up to **Android 15+**.

---

## 📥 1. Downloading the APK

1. Go to the **[Sonance GitHub Actions](https://github.com/Jttacalig/sonance/actions)** page.
2. Click on the latest completed workflow run named **`Build Android APK (Android 8.0+)`**.
3. Scroll down to the **Artifacts** section at the bottom.
4. Download the **`Sonance-Android-APK`** zip file.
5. Extract the `.zip` file to get `Sonance-Universal-Release.apk`.
6. Transfer or download the `.apk` directly to your Android device (via browser, Google Drive, WhatsApp, or USB cable).

---

## ⚙️ 2. Enabling "Install Unknown Apps"

Because Sonance is distributed directly (sideloaded) rather than through Google Play Store, Android requires one-time permission to install APK files.

### For Android 8.0 - Android 15+:
1. Open the downloaded `Sonance-Universal-Release.apk` on your phone (using Chrome, Files, or your file manager).
2. If prompted with *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
   - Tap **Settings**.
   - Toggle **Allow from this source** to **ON**.
   - Press the **Back** button.
3. Tap **Install**.

---

## 🎧 3. Features on Android:

- **Offline Library**: Instant access to your downloaded songs stored locally in high fidelity.
- **Background Playback**: Keep listening when you minimize the app, lock your screen, or switch to other apps.
- **Lock Screen & Notification Controls**: Pause, play, and skip tracks with artwork right from your Android notification drawer and lockscreen.
- **Direct Downloader & Multi-Platform**: Extract audio from YouTube, SoundCloud, Instagram, TikTok, and direct media URLs.
- **Local File Import**: Import songs from your phone's storage or SD card via the Android File Picker.
- **Customization & Equalizer**: 5-band Equalizer, Custom Theme Accents, and Ambient Glow effects.
